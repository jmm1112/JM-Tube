JM TUBE CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 1.2.2
DATE: 2026-11-15
STATUS: stable

HIGHLIGHTS
- Unified Storage Hierarchy: Consolidated all app downloads and data under a single root directory `/storage/emulated/0/Download/JM Tube/` with dedicated subdirectories for `Music` (`/storage/emulated/0/Download/JM Tube/Music`), `Videos` (`/storage/emulated/0/Download/JM Tube/Videos`), and `Backups` (`/storage/emulated/0/Download/JM Tube/Backups`)
- Redesigned Sync Screen: Brand-new modular layout featuring a top-level sync status card with instant "Sync now" triggering and live sync timestamps
- Devices-First Hierarchy: Linked devices are now highlighted at the top of the sync screen, giving immediate visibility into active devices on your network
- Dedicated Device Sheet: Tap any linked device to open its dedicated configuration sheet to view status, adjust remote playback permissions, or remove the device
- Simplified Sync Setup: Streamlined entry paths to easily "Create a group" or "Join a group" with intuitive "Show a code" and "Scan its code" pairing workflows
- Play On Remote Control Sheet Tidy: Repositioned "Manage linked devices" into the header and added a prominent playing device status banner directly above the current track
- Playback Status Clarifications: New granular status indicators ("Playing here", "Paused here", "Paused on [Device]") for seamless remote playback monitoring
