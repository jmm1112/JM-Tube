JM TUBE CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 1.2.3
DATE: 2026-11-15
STATUS: stable

HIGHLIGHTS
- Linked Push on Change: Linked devices receive playlists, likes, channel subscriptions, and watch history changes in near real-time while both devices are actively open in the foreground via LAN/WebSocket channel with burst debounce protection.
- Remote Playback Elsewhere Hint & Quick Transfer: When another linked device is playing media and local playback is idle, the mini player displays a remote hint with a "Play here" button to seamlessly transfer and resume playback on this device with one tap.
- Handoff Timeout Extension & Slow-Start Resilience: Extended remote handoff response timeout to 25s, ensuring slow-buffering or high-latency media transitions on remote targets never report false failure or cause simultaneous playback.
- Android TV "Play on" Integration: Android TV navigation rail now displays active remote playback device and title, opening the "Play on" remote controller directly with quick shortcuts to manage linked devices.
