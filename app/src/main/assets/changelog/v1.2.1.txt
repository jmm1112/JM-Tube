JM TUBE CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 1.2.1
DATE: 2026-11-15
STATUS: stable

HIGHLIGHTS
- Flow-Sync 2.0 Multi-Device Sync & Remote Control: End-to-end encrypted device linking protocol with QR code pairing, 12-word recovery seed phrase backup, real-time sync across devices, linked device management, and "Play on Device" remote streaming control
- Modular Video Transcripts: Full interactive transcript sheet with instant keyword search, language selector menu, real-time auto-follow scrolling synchronized with video playback, and TXT/SRT export
- Hierarchical Nested Comment Reply Threads: Dedicated nested comment reply threads with expandable branches, author badges, and fluid drawer animations
- Comment Media Support: Rich GIF and image rendering support in comment sections with optional Wi-Fi only restriction
- Landscape Fullscreen Side Panels: Configurable side panel width (Compact, Standard, Wide) to browse comments, transcripts, or playback queue side-by-side in fullscreen landscape mode
- 360° Spherical Video Motion & Drag: Gyroscope motion sensor tracking and touch drag velocity controls for immersive spherical video viewing
- Atmospheric Video Ambient Glow: Dynamic atmospheric lighting effect matching video colors rendered behind the player, with battery saver and immersive scroll dimming controls
- Media Player Notification Widget Like Button: Added Like/Favorite button directly to the Android notification panel's media player widget with real-time background sync, allowing users to like or unlike the currently playing track without opening the app
- Shorts Auto-Scroll Support: Added auto-scroll capability for the Shorts section that automatically scrolls and transitions to the next video when playback ends, with seamless repeat mode handling and comment/description sheet pause protection
- Digital Wellbeing & Daily Watch Limits: Comprehensive daily watch limits with customizable countdown warnings, Time's Up screen lockout, and background DailyLimitGuard enforcement across video, music, and shorts
- Highlight New Settings ("Keep the dot"): Dynamic feature badges and dots across settings categories to highlight newly introduced settings until visited
- Gradient Loading Shimmer Placeholders: Fluid animated skeleton gradient placeholders for video thumbnails and cards matching the active color theme
- Feed & Watched Content Settings: Support for counting imported Takeout videos as watched, configurable watched progress thresholds, toggleable Home chips, Home new videos button, subscription upcoming videos, posts chip, and ungrouped channel section
- AI Channel Filter & Reporting Integration: Built-in AiSList community filtering with options to hide or label suspected AI-generated channels, custom list source support, and one-tap AI channel reporting from video menus
- SponsorBlock Enhancements: Added Skip button styles, skip notices, highlight jump button, and fine-grained action handling for SponsorBlock categories
- Paid Promotion Chip UI: Displays translucent overlay chip on videos containing paid promotions or sponsorships
- Mini Player Geometric Shape Morphing: 35 morphing geometric cover shapes (Circle, Squircle, Pill, Rounded Hexagon, Stars 4-8 pt, Flowers 4-8 pt, Sunny, Cookies 4-12 pt, Clovers 4-8 pt, Heart, Ghost, Arch, Wiggle, Cutout) with configurable resting shape and motion modes (On Play, Always, Static)
- Mini Player Appearance & Actions: Configurable progress indicators (Ring, Fill, Edge, Line, Wavy Line), background styles (Tinted, Vivid, App Theme, Cover Colours, Black, Outlined), customizable action buttons (up to 3 buttons), and configurable tap & long-press behaviors
- Lyrics Cadence & Glow Animation: Fluid character-by-character cadence animations with note glow, blur background lines, customizable lyrics text alignment, and solid/gradient background styling
- Queue Multi-Selection & Batch Actions: Multi-track selection in playback queue with batch remove, play next, enqueue, add to playlist, and batch download
- Expanded Player Background Styles: Blur Gradient, Dynamic Moving Colours, Cover Colours, Geometric Shapes, and True Black player backgrounds
- Split Transport Controls: Optional split playback transport controls in the music player
- Pause Crossfade Quick Action: Temporarily pause audio crossfade until app restart directly from song menu
- Animated Artwork Battery Optimization: Efficient pause and decoder retention when player sheet is collapsed, behind lyrics, or covered by the queue
- Continuous Track Title & Artist Marquee: Smooth looping text marquee on music player header with stillness pause when hidden
- Gesture Chapter Skipping: Two-finger double-tap gesture to seamlessly jump forward or backward between video chapters
- Mini Player Synced Lyrics: Real-time synced lyric lines directly within the music mini player bar
- Portrait Idle Progress Strip: Ultra-thin progress indicator displayed at the bottom of the video player when controls are hidden
- Stats for Nerds Diagnostic Overlay: Comprehensive real-time player diagnostics displaying video format, resolution, audio loudness, dropped frames, decoder, and viewport metrics
- Lyrics Share Card & Image Export: Create and export customized, high-resolution aesthetic lyric quote image cards with classic, poster, and expressive shape layouts
- Extended Lyrics Providers: Integrated AMLL (word-synced TTML) and NetEase Cloud Music lyric providers into the automated lyrics fallback pipeline
- Google Takeout Import Fix: Fixed invisible/blank song titles and channel names on imported Takeout history and playlists
- Multilingual Action Prefix Stripping: Improved Takeout parsing for non-English account exports (Spanish, German, French, Hindi, etc.)
- Automatic Fallback Titles: Integrated fallback display titles ("Video" / "Music Track") and channel names ("YouTube") across all UI list rows
